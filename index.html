POKéMON EMERALD GUIDE

🎮 Controls

Movement: Arrow keys or W, A, S, D

Running Shoes: Hold Shift (or B) while walking

Interact / A Button: Z, Space, or Enter

Cancel / B Button: X or Backspace

In-game Menu: C or M or Escape

🌲 How to Play

1. Start in Littleroot Town and inspect Professor Birch's Bag to choose your starter (Treecko, Torchic, or Mudkip)!

2. Talk to Mom near your house to heal and receive Running Shoes!

3. Travel north along Route 101 through the tall grass to encounter wild Pokémon (Poochyena, Zigzagoon, Wurmple, Taillow, Ralts)!

4. Battle wild Pokémon to earn EXP, level up, and evolve your starter at Level 16!

5. Visit Oldale Town to use the Pokémon Center (Nurse Joy heals your party for free) and buy Pokéballs at the Mart!

6. Explore northern Petalburg Woods and reach the ancient Sky Shrine to challenge the Legendary Dragon Rayquaza!

💾 Saving Your Game

Press C to open the in-game menu and choose SAVE. Your progress, Pokémon team, items, and catches are saved directly to your browser!
